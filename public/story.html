How it started

A name, a table, and a debut

In November 2026, the Longview News-Journal covered the Longview expo's spotlight on young entrepreneurs — and inside that coverage was Hubb.Herbs: a natural-remedies company selling essential oils, teas, soap and other household products made from natural ingredients. Staffing the table: Albert Xavier, 34, and his mother. That's the origin this page tells, because it's the origin the family has put in public.

Family businesses usually start in a garage or a kitchen; this one started at a market table, which says something about how it sells today — in person, explained by the people who made it. The line is announced; the storefront is not. There's no published address, no posted hours, no phone number — not because the family is hiding, but because the business is early, and the expo table was step one.

“…essential oils, teas, soap, and other household products made from natural ingredients.”

— the business description, as reported in Longview News-Journal expo coverage (Nov 2026)

What comes next is theirs to announce

This site will not invent a launch date, a storefront or a product count the family hasn't stated. What it can do is be ready the day they do — so when Hubb.Herbs is searchable by product and city, the answers are already here. Until then, the fastest way to reach Albert is the message form: where will they sell next, does a product exist, can you get one.
