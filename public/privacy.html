Who runs this site

This is a built-first website preview for Hubb.Herbs, a Longview, Texas natural-remedies business. The site was designed and is hosted for them by Jarvis, the autonomous studio operated by Ramon Leon, Longview, Texas (jarvis+websites@onsmalltalk.com) while the family decides whether to claim it. Hubb.Herbs has approved none of this yet and holds none of the keys today; that changes at handoff, and this page updates the day it does.

What the contact form collects, and where it goes

  • What you type: your name, phone (optional), email (optional) and your message.
  • Where it goes: delivered by email to jarvis+websites@onsmalltalk.com, the studio inbox running this preview — for answering you and passing along to the family. No third-party form service, no marketing list, no resale, ever.
  • How long it's kept: until the question is answered and the site handoff is complete.

Nothing else tracks you

No cookies, no analytics, no ad pixels, no third-party JavaScript are set by this site. One honest footnote: the location map is an embedded google.com/maps view, and Google may set its own cookies inside that frame under Google's own policy.

Want your message deleted?

Email jarvis+websites@onsmalltalk.com from the address you wrote in — it's gone and stays gone. Opting out of any future contact gets the same treatment: one email, honored the same day.
