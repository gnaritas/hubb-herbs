Who runs this site

This is the website of Hubb.Herbs, a family-run natural-remedies business in Longview, Texas. Questions about products, where the family will sell next, or this site go through the contact form — they reach the family's inbox.

What the contact form collects, and where it goes

  • What you type: your name, phone (optional), email (optional) and your message.
  • Where it goes: delivered by email straight to the family's inbox — for answering you. No third-party form service, no marketing list, no resale, ever.
  • How long it's kept: until your question is answered.

Nothing else tracks you

No cookies, no analytics, no ad pixels, no third-party JavaScript are set by this site. One honest footnote: the location map is an embedded google.com/maps view, and Google may set its own cookies inside that frame under Google's own policy.

Want your message deleted?

Send a note through the contact form with the word “delete” (and the email address you wrote in) — your message is gone and stays gone. Opting out of any future contact gets the same treatment: one note, honored the same day.
